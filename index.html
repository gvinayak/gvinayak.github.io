Hi there! Just call me Vin.

I am a Senior AI Researcher at Chan Zuckerberg Biohub, where I work within the AI Research org. My research is in machine learning at scale: optimized training, data curation, and rigorous benchmarking, applied today to AI for Science through multimodal foundation models for proteins and genomics. Earlier, I worked on web-scale data mining, time-series forecasting, reasoning with language models, and proactive conversational agents, with applications in healthcare and drug discovery.

Before Biohub, I was an AI Researcher at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL); a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science, University of Washington, where I worked with Tim Althoff; and an AI Scientist at IBM Research. I have also held positions as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon and an R&D Intern at Siemens Healthcare. I have led the AI efforts on projects sponsored by the U.S. DOE, NIH, and NSF, and my work has been featured in IBM blogs, recognized as a runner-up in NASSCOM's AI Gamechangers 2022, and nominated for several doctoral dissertation awards. One of my papers was highlighted by an AI influencer as "one of the biggest revelations of 2024". A full list of my publications is available on my Google Scholar profile.
